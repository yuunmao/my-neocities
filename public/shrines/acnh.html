Animal Crossing New Horizons

I didn't get a Switch until 2024, so I'm a bit of a latecomer to the ACNH community. Never played the DS games either, so New Horizons is my first foray into the world, and it's been such a delight!


A big reason I came to love ACNH was because I was able to do things I'd never do irl. I can run around! Jump! Hang out in the forest! Plant a garden! Lowkey this game even cured my bug phobia... I still don't like 'em but I also don't panic the way I used to.


I've always been a casual gamer. I'll take month long breaks, and I'm too lazy to time travel and make up for it. As a result, there's still some loose ends I need to tie up on my island.


But without further ado, let me show you around~.

Welcome to Pandora

I quickly learned that island themes were a big thing for players, but I don't really have one. If anything, I could possibly fall under fairycore? I do have a lot of glowy spots.

Native Flora: Chyrsanthemums | Cherries

In general, I loosely headcanon that the entire island is some sort of medical facility. They send me strange and mentally ill villagers because they can't live peacefully in the greater society. Residual effects from my menhera phase and whatnot.

Meet the Hostages Locals

I mostly enjoy letting my villagers come and go, but these are the ones who are stuck with me forever >:)

Pate was my starter Peppy villager! I maxed our friendship so she was the first to ever ask me to move out ToT I agreed because yesss bestie, of course you gotta chase your dreams!! Instant regret :/ Took me a year and a half to find her again and you best believe she is stuck here this time. Diagnosis: severe anxiety disorder.

Claudia was my first autofill villager. I was kinda pissed at first because she was ruining the all blue vibes I was chasing, but somehow she grew on me... She's snooty but she makes me feel cared for uwu. Diagnosis: she's the senior nurse on duty!

Lucky was found completely unexpected on a villager hunt. I never actually planned to have him, but once he arrived, he kept saying how tasty everything on the island was. EVERYTHING. So I was like, oh, I can sooo work with this. And now he has the worst backstory of my villagers. Diagnosis: abandonment issues and murder.

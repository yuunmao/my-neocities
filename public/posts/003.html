Subject: Trip to Grayson Highlands

First of all, HAPPY HALLOWEEN!!! Second of all, I just had my most successful trip since surgery and I’m very emotional about it!!

The main complication that arose from my thyroidectomy was that it paralyzed my vocal cords and over time left me with a rather narrow airway. Even speedwalking can leave me gasping for air. It really sucks as someone who (1) lives along a mountain range and (2) loves to travel. But hey, it could've been worse. I could've been an athlete and had my whole career ended. Point is, agreeing to a trip to the Grayson Highlands was scarier than I wanted to admit. I'm so glad I did though.

When I visited the Scottish Highlands in 2022, I regretted not bringing my DSLR so that was one of the first things I made sure to pack this time. Sure I haven't used it since graduating high school but I'll probably remember as I go. (The answer was kinda.)

Medical concerns aside, I was also a little worried because I had read that the park was past peak. Luckily there were still a handful of gorgeous spots among the dead ones.

The Rhododendron Trail is considered a moderate hike, but only about a mile long. Most importantly, it's the trail with the highest chance of pony sightings >:3 I had to risk it.

In my head, I know that walking slower will curb any wheezing, but my legs just don’t ever want to listen. Thanks to the nature of the trip (ha ha?), I was finally actually doing that. Guess what? Hardly a wheeze. Crazy how that works.

I often bemoan how much surgery nerfed me, but I’m realizing my new self isn’t that> much different. She just needs a little more time to heal.

On this trip I also learned that gravy comes in white, tried a Cook Out milkshake (that shit was just ice cream, let's be real), searched for fairy stones (found 3!), and went to Cracker Barrel for the first time!

I am very much a child of the concrete, and have only gotten more attached since surgery. The idea of rural living unnerved me, but life out there was more simple and peaceful than I expected. Travel is so important in that way. I really really missed it. I feel like a spark has been renewed and hope that planning more trips will be possible now <3

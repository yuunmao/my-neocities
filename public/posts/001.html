Wow I forgot about those. Man I took pretty notes. Wonder if I remember any of it.

And now I’m here, on neocities. Time is just a flat circle fr :/

But then again, this is just my personal site that I work on for funsies, so does it really matter?

As much as I’ve always detested school, I do love learning. I think I’m at a good point with HTML/CSS. The remaining gaps in my knowledge will probably be filled the longer I maintain this site. The main thing stopping me from finishing the other pages are because I need to make the assets. For the shrines, I wanna draw stained glass windows that glow when you hover over them :3

Because yes, I am aware the arrow indicating the current blog post you are reading disappears once you’ve clicked elsewhere.
